# 1 Project Description
Northstar Potash Processing Facility, located in Saskatchewan Canada has the goal of extracting processing and dispatching potash (KCl) which is used int the production of fertilizer.

# 2 Project Objectives
Construction and commissioning of fully functional potash processing facility

# 3 Plant Process Overview
`this would be prepared by the lead process engineer`
- Raw ore receiving
- Crushing and slurrying the ore
- desliming (removing clay)
- Crushing 2
- flotation / separation
- drying and sizing the potash cristals
- Product storing and shipping

# 5 Project Execution Phases
- Engineering
  - Engineering Design
    - developing the process design
    - process equipment requirements are determined
    - dry material conveying and piping - designed to transport the material between key process equipment based on the process requirements
    - pumps and motors - designed to serve the conveying
    - sensors and control loops - designed to control pumps and motors in their service to the major process equipment and the drivers of material conveying
    - support process circuits - such as sumps, scrubbers, reclaim is designed and integrated into process design
    - power supply - designed to drive everything along with power distribution
    - hvac - to maintain air
    - water - different types of water designed to rout to major process equipment and stuff
    - building and earthworks designed to hold everything
  - Engineering Packages
    - Packaging
      - different process components from major process equipment down to pipes, cable, ducts,...; All are assigned to different packages
      - they can be broken by discipline, zone, or major items can have stand-alone packages
      - each package will carry engineering requirements that need to be met either package wide or for each individual item
    - Issuing for bids and selecting vendors per package
- Procurement
  - Issued packages are procured from vendor, engineering specification and package documents are consulted in this phase
  - Schedule of procurement is tied in with construction schedule
- Construction
  - Earthworks and buildings are made
  - Equipment is being installed - for example a pump is mounted on a base plate and grouted in place
  - Connections to conveying is made
  - Things are built as it was designed with potential (red line) adjustments that can be made with engineerings consultation
- Mechanical Completion 
  - ensuring things are installed and connected properly
  - ITR are run
  - Walkdowns with engineers present need to be done on subsystem basis to allow pre- commissioning to begin
- Pre-commissioning
  - after mechanical completion has been verified we need to check if equipment runs if subsystems that can be tested can run
- Commissioning
  - systems that have had all their subsystems pre-commissioned are now tested
  - we can do a dry run, run water only to make sure everything operates
- Start-up - we are running tests with product
- Handover to Operations - system with all the documentation is handed over to operations
`It is impoortant to note that a mine could be operating and piling raw ore while the crushing facility is being built. There are times for example skipping would have to stop because the shaft needs to be used for something else. These are all optimizations of scheduling.`
