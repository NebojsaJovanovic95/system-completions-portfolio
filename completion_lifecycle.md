# Typical project completion lifecycle
1. Construction - Equipment installation, piping, electrical installation, instrumentation and construction inspections
2. Mechanical Completion - Verify installation against approved engineering documents, inspections, and applicable acceptance criteria
3. Pre-commissioning - Activities such as cleaning, flushing, pressure testing, continuity testing, and other discipline-specific checks
4. Commissioning - Functional testing and verification that equipment and subsystems operate as intended
5. Start-up - Introduction of process materials and controlled transition to operational service
6. Handover and Operations - Formal transfer of the completed facilities, outstanding items, documentation, and operational responsibilities
